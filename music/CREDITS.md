# Music sources & licenses (Salah's City Journey)

All melodies, rhythms and arrangements are original. The reference recording below was used ONLY to analyse tempo/rhythm/texture and is NOT included in the game or previews.

## Reference (analysis only, not distributed)
- "اغاني بحرية من تراث الغوص في الخليج العربي" — channel تراثيات, YouTube dIjpteValKs (nahham Salem Al-Allan). Not included in this repository. Findings: rhythmic cycle ≈0.93 s / 1.86 s (≈129 BPM pulse, later ≈103), dense group clapping, sung chorus + solo nahham, quarter-tone (bayati-like) pitch use.

## Synthesised (no samples)
- Oud plucks: Karplus–Strong physical model + body resonance.
- Violin: bowed sawtooth with vibrato and body filtering.
- Chorus / nahham-like vocal line: additive formant synthesis (vowels "o"/"a"), no real voices.
- Sea waves: filtered noise.
